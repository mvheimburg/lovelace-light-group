# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-22

- Group household lights by room, with one responsive card per floor or zone.
- Toggle individual lights, adjust brightness, open HA light controls, or turn off the zone.
- Confirm actions against HA state, prevent overlapping requests and show failures.
- Configure all bindings and appearance in a visual Lovelace editor.
- Support English/Bokmål, Default/Bubble appearances and six color schemes.
- Include independent HACS packaging, browser tests, CI and versioned releases.
